    About the author

    Kira Ashvale

    Kira Ashvale writes paranormal romance for readers who want the rejection to hurt and the grovel to be worth it. Her wolves live in snowbound mountain towns where the packs keep old laws, the lanterns remember every bond, and the heroine always gets the final say.

    The Midwinter Packs is her first series: five interconnected books, one per winter, each with its own couple and its own happy ending.
